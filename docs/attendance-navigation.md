# Attendance workspace

Attendance now groups the existing actions into four sections for HR, Administrator, Principal and President:

- **Upload Data:** the five-column raw biometric CSV is the primary action. **Other formats** opens the existing combined absence/late/early monthly upload. The older device/event CSV importer remains under a collapsed Advanced option.
- **Attendance Register:** department register and downloads, manual entry, department bulk corrections, reviewed daily records, and optional raw-punch evidence.
- **Late & Early Review:** the existing monthly late review, authorized CL/LOP decisions, and department editing for early-going/time exceptions. The 25-minute monthly allowance is unchanged.
- **HR Confirmation:** select a month and load current backend status. Pending attendance, leave or late decisions disable the confirmation button. Changing month clears the loaded status. Finalize/reopen still use the existing backend role, module, company, source-hash and payroll-lock checks.

Accountants see Register, Late & Early Review, and read-only HR Confirmation. Manager/Director roles retain their existing attendance and late-review viewing flows without company-wide raw-register or HR confirmation controls. Staff self-service navigation is unchanged.

Keyboard: Left/Right Arrow moves between the available tabs; Home/End selects the first/last. Tab selection resets when switching company. All old backend routes remain intact; this is a navigation and presentation change, with no database migration.
