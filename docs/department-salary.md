# Department-wise Salary Edit

With Administrator, Principal or President access, open **Staff Master → Salary Edit — department wise**.

1. Choose a department and effective date, then enter a reason.
2. Select individual staff or all staff in the department.
3. Enter each selected employee's salary components. Gross and Cum Gross calculate automatically.
4. Save the selected rows together.

Staff without a salary receive an initial salary version. Staff with an existing salary receive a recommendation, which follows **Assessments & Increments → Review cost → Approve salary**. The independent accountant reviews the recommendation before administrator approval. Existing salary history is retained.

The revision date must follow the latest salary date shown. Locked payroll periods cannot be changed. A stale salary, changed department or invalid row blocks the whole batch. Reopening the editor loads current data. Changing department clears unsaved entries; unselected rows are not saved.
