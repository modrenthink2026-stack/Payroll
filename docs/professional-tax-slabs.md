# Automatic Professional Tax slabs

With company automatic PT enabled:

| Earned monthly Cum Gross before deductions | Monthly PT |
| --- | --- |
| Up to ₹15,000 | ₹0 |
| Above ₹15,000 up to ₹20,000 | ₹150 |
| Above ₹20,000 | ₹200 |

The calculation includes HOD/Coordinator allowances and consolidated salary. Payroll applies the slab once to the month's earned total, including the effect of loss of pay. Salary-entry estimates assume a full paid month.

Enable the company policy and choose its first applicable month in **Company settings → Edit settings**. The policy is company-specific. Pre-policy months and manual-mode companies keep their Tax Inputs defaults. Review the applicable jurisdiction and any individual exemptions through the employer's normal payroll review.

A salary Professional Tax override takes priority, including an explicit zero. Leave the override blank for the company's automatic/default calculation. Existing saved payslips are not rewritten; recalculate and review unlocked payroll after policy changes.

Reference checked: Telangana Commercial Taxes Department, First Schedule, entry 1: https://ptax.tgct.gov.in/tgportal/AllActs/APPT/APPTSchedule.aspx
