# Salary details

Salary details follow this order:

Basic | DA | HRA | CCA | Allowances | Gross | HOD/Coordinator Allowances | Cum Gross

Gross = Basic + DA + HRA + CCA + Allowances.
Cum Gross = Gross + HOD/Coordinator Allowances.

Totals are calculated, not entered. Existing Other allowance amounts remain under Allowances. New CCA and HOD/Coordinator components default to zero until reviewed amounts are entered.

Set initial salary in Staff Master. Subsequent changes use the existing dated increment recommendation, independent accountant review and administrator approval workflow. Each component is prorated by paid days and the applicable salary version. Payroll's total earnings amount is Cum Gross; the payslip shows both totals without adding subtotals into earnings again. Existing saved payslips retain their historical amounts.

The salary CSV accepts `cca` and `hod_allowance` alongside the existing `basic`, `da`, `hra` and `other` columns. The full personnel roster remains separate from salary entry.
