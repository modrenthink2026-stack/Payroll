# Department and staff shift timings

Open **Staff Master → Assign shift timings** with HR or full company access.

1. Choose a department, Unassigned, or All departments.
2. Search by staff name, code or employee reference.
3. Select individual staff or select all matching staff. Review the selected count.
4. Enter shift name, start/end time, break and grace minutes, then Save.

Changing department clears the selection. Searching keeps previously selected staff selected, including staff hidden by the search. Only active staff can be selected, up to 500 per save. Invalid or unavailable selections prevent the whole save.

An end earlier than the start means the shift ends the next day. Breaks must be shorter than the shift. Assignment changes subsequent attendance entries; saved attendance and its shift snapshots remain unchanged. This action does not recalculate past attendance or payroll.

Use **Staff Master → Edit** to adjust one employee's shift separately. Department assignment applies only to the selected existing staff; it is not a default automatically inherited by future recruits.
