# Company logins and employee logins

## Users who are not employees

With full company access, open **Company settings → Create system user**. Enter the person's name, a unique username (lowercase letters, numbers and dots), company role, and a temporary password of at least 12 characters. Share credentials privately. The person signs in with that username and must set a personal password at first login.

This creates company access without a Staff Master record, salary, attendance or leave balances. Use it for an external Accountant, HR operator or company Administrator. The **User / Manager** role has limited company access; employee self-service requires a Staff Master record. Existing role permissions apply within the selected company. **System users** lists these accounts and pending setup. Repeating creation with the same name, username and role resumes incomplete setup; it never resets an existing password. Choose another username when one is already reserved with different details.

## Employee logins

Payroll Manager assigns a unique name-based username when the Staff Master record is saved. Duplicate names receive distinct suffixes. Personnel email remains contact information.

1. Create the Staff Master record.
2. Activate the staff login and share its username privately.
3. With full company access, open **Company settings → Assign employee role**.
4. Select the activated username and the authorized role, then Save.

The list shows username, staff name and employee reference/code to distinguish similar names. Only active linked staff in the current company are available. System-user accounts are separate and cannot be linked to Staff Master. Existing staff usernames and passwords remain unchanged. New staff still set a personal password at first login. Existing email-based accounts retain their identity.
